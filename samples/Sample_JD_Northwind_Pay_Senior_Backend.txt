Senior Backend Engineer - Payments Platform
Northwind Pay | Bengaluru, India (Hybrid, 3 days in office) | Full-time
About the role
Northwind Pay processes card and UPI payments for 40,000 merchants. Our Payments Platform team builds the services that move money reliably: ledgers, settlements and payouts. You will design and own event-driven backend services that handle millions of transactions a day.
What you will do
Design, build and operate event-driven microservices for settlements and payouts
Build and maintain Kafka-based pipelines for payment events
Own services end to end: design, deployment on Kubernetes, monitoring and on-call
Improve reliability and latency of high-volume REST APIs
Mentor engineers and lead technical design reviews
Work with finance and compliance teams on reconciliation and audit needs

5+ years of backend development experience

Kafka or a similar event streaming platform

Kubernetes and Docker in production
REST API design
AWS
